Security, IPC & Privacy

  • Updated CertVerifierServiceImpl::EnableNetworkAccess() so that calling it more than once is treated as a bad Mojo message and immediately terminates the remote. (f27de9c)
    SecurityNetwork
  • Relanded DOS reserved device name validation for relative paths under Windows to prevent directory sandbox bypasses. (27bb7c8)
    SecurityWindows

    ExtensionsCryptoPrivacy

    CryptoSecurity

    BrowserPDF

    AutofillBlink

    BlinkPolicy

    TabsUIBuild

    BrowserUI
