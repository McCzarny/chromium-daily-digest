    BlinkHTML

Security, Content & Android WebView

    AndroidSecurity
  • Implemented a server-authentication mechanism on client Mach channels via NamedPlatformChannel and ChannelMac to prevent bootstrap name squatting vulnerabilities on macOS. (13391ee)
    SecuritymacOS
  • Introduced embedder hooks via ContentBrowserClient::IsContentSchemeURLNavigationAllowed() to block unauthorized web-initiated navigations to unexported internal Android content providers. (50dd531)
    AndroidSecurityBrowser

Performance & Base Utilities

    AndroidUIPerformance

    BasePerformance
  • Gated expensive PartitionAlloc validation routines behind EXPENSIVE_DCHECKS_ARE_ON instead of standard DCHECK_IS_ON() to reduce CQ release trybuilder CPU overhead. (ea7dba5)
    BasePerformanceTesting
