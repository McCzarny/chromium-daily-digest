Overview

Week 32 of 2026 brought major architectural progress across Chromium, headlined by the removal of synchronous legacy IPC and the continued decoupling of monolithic Browser methods under Project Bedrock. Security teams enforced origin-keyed process isolation and service worker blocking for privileged WebContents while Android saw key ANR optimizations.
