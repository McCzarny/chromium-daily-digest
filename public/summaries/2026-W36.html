Chromium Weekly: 2026 Week 36

Overview

This week in Chromium development saw major rendering milestones including the default enablement of JPEG XL image decoding, CSS counter style symbol functions, and significant canvas flush optimizations. Security hardening advanced with post-quantum KEM support, renderer initiator token tracking, and Windows WSL path containment. In total, 3,217 relevant commits out of 4,088 total across 6 days were analyzed.
