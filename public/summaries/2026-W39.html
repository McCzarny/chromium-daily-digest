Overview

This week in Chromium featured the removal of the Fenced Frames web platform API alongside notable media and rendering changes across Blink. Significant memory and IPC optimizations landed alongside continuous hardening for DevTools, enterprise controls, and renderer download security. Across the 6 days analyzed, 2,386 relevant commits were processed out of 3,832 total commits.
