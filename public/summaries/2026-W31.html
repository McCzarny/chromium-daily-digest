Chromium Weekly: 2026 Week 31

Overview

This week in Chromium development, major architectural modernizations took place alongside significant security enhancements and mobile UX features. Key highlights include migrating tab identifiers to 64-bit integers, transitioning internal infrastructure to C++20 standard library views, and introducing Gemini AI integrations on Android. Across 6 days, 2966 relevant commits out of 3796 total were analyzed.
