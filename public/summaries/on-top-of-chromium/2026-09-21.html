Chromium Digest: 2026-09-21

Overview

On 2026-09-21, the Chromium main branch received 693 commits, of which 545 were relevant after filtering. The day included several breaking changes to stable public APIs: privatized ClipboardEndpoint constructors in the content public API, removal of GLES2Interface::DidGpuSwitch(), a target_network parameter added to the WebSocket NetworkContext Mojo API, and removal of the RWS cookie exclusion reason from net::CookieInclusionStatus and the DevTools protocol. First commit: (2f7bcb7), last commit: (02b40e4).
