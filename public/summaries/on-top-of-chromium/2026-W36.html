Chromium Weekly: 2026 Week 36

Overview

This week in Chromium development saw notable architectural progress with the ongoing Project Bedrock UI modularization, standardization of browser window feature accessors, and significant enterprise extension API additions. Core subsystems also received security hardening around navigation commits, request headers, and cryptographic primitives. Across the 7 days, 3,259 relevant commits out of 4,130 total were analyzed for desktop environments on Windows and macOS.
