Browser UI & Architecture

  • Removed deprecated direct accessor methods on the Browser class (Browser::browser_actions() and Browser::command_controller()), migrating internal usages to BrowserActions::From() and BrowserCommandController::From(). (672dcac) (8c1fcec)
    BrowserUI

    BrowserNavigation

    TabsUI

Networking, Security & Privacy

    NetworkStorage

    PrivacyStorage

    NetworkSSL

    BaseI18n
