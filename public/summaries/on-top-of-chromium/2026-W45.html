Chromium Weekly: 2026 Week 45

Overview

Week 45 of 2026 brought substantial public API refactorings across the browser and base libraries, alongside critical security hardenings in IPC, protocol handling, and file system boundaries. Core changes include Mojo validator de-virtualization, the deprecation of legacy TabFeatures accessors in favor of tab data abstractions, and stricter enterprise device trust enforcement. Across the week, there were 3,745 relevant commits out of 4,797 total across 5 days.
