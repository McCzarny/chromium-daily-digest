Overview

This week in Chromium development focused heavily on Project Bedrock modularization, moving key browser subsystems toward BrowserWindowInterface. Essential security hardening was delivered across Windows sandbox paths and extension protocol handlers, alongside startup performance optimizations for layout and storage initialization. Overall, 3128 relevant commits out of 3935 total across 6 days were analyzed for desktop environments.
