Overview

On 2026-11-05, 1174 commits landed on the main branch (917 relevant to embedders, browser, networking, security, and developer-facing infrastructure). Notable themes include several hard removals from stable public APIs (Mojo message validation, cc/viz trilinear filtering, TabFeatures getters, pref_member.h language-tag specialization, ContentBrowserClient::GetUserBrowserContext()), a substantial Mojo serialization optimization, security hardening of protocol handlers and the browser-actuator transport, and WebUI toolbar/omnibox work on Windows and macOS.
