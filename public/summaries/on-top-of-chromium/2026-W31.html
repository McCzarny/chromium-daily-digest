Security & Sandboxing

  • ⚠️BREAKING CHANGE Updated ChromeContentBrowserClient::IsJitDisabledForSite() to check ChildProcessSecurityPolicy::IsWebSafeScheme() instead of restricting checks solely to HTTP/HTTPS URLs. (ffb88da)
    SecurityBrowser
  • ⚠️Blocked web-initiated navigations targeting custom direct launch schemes (google-chrome:, chromium:) within Blink and added browser-side validation to terminate compromised renderers. (5abdd77)
