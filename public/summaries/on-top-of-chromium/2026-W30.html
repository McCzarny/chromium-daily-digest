Overview

During Week 30 of 2026, Chromium development focused on modularizing the monolithic chrome/browser/ui architecture, expanding Extension API capabilities, and hardening platform security. 2882 relevant commits were identified out of 3766 total commits across 7 days, bringing significant refactoring to browser window interfaces and desktop tab handling on Windows and macOS.
