Chromium Weekly: 2026 Week 43

Overview

Chromium development in Week 43 of 2026 brought substantial public API teardowns, deep security hardening, and performance optimizations across Windows and macOS, representing 3,956 relevant commits out of 4,867 total across 7 days. Key updates include the removal of legacy Shared Storage and First-Party Sets embedder interfaces, breaking changes across ContentBrowserClient and ui::TreeModel, and format upgrades for DeclarativeNetRequest rulesets. Embedders also benefit from substantial lookup speedups in base::FeatureList, stricter IPC and process isolation, and expanded Device Bound Session Credentials (DBSC) support.
