Overview

This week in Chromium development, major architectural modernization continued through Project Bedrock with widespread migrations to BrowserWindowInterface and test framework modularization. Key updates include additions to post-quantum cryptography, new extension APIs like chrome.publicSuffix, and UI performance enhancements around theme traversals and frame eviction.
